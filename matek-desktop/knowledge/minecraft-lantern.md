# Minecraft: regular iron lantern crafting recipe

Source: https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/taking-inventory--lantern
Reference checked: 2026-10-13. Scope: standard regular iron lantern, not modded recipes.

Ingredients: one torch and eight iron nuggets.
Use a crafting table's 3 by 3 grid. Put the torch in the centre slot and an iron nugget in each of the eight surrounding slots. This crafts one regular lantern.
For a soul lantern, replace the regular torch with a soul torch; keep the eight iron nuggets.
Modpacks or custom data packs may change recipes, so use their own instructions when applicable.
